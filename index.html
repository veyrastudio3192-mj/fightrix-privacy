FIGHTRIX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

FIGHTRIX ("the game") is made by Yoddha Studios. This page explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress (Yatra stages, stars, unlocked bosses), settings and saved replays are kept only on your phone, inside the game's own storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Internet and local network

  • The game asks for network permission only for the ONLINE (same Wi-Fi) mode. In that mode your phone talks directly to the other players' phones on the same Wi-Fi network to share button presses during a fight. Nothing is sent to the internet or to our servers — we do not have any servers.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone. Because we collect no data, no data of children is collected either.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs. Voices and sounds were created with ElevenLabs and are part of the game files; no data is sent to ElevenLabs when you play.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: write to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
